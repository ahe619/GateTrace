# Collaborator handoff checklist

At the recorded handoff stage, this was a local candidate repository. Remote initialization, upload and delivery remained separate steps.

1. Read the README's claim boundaries, then RESULTS. Describe the strong baseline's measured gains within the evidence for its established components.
2. Run the CPU cache self-check, then inspect standard MOTChallenge-output scoring and the evidence interface. Users obtain original GT, images and weights separately under official terms.
3. Consult FROZEN_CODE_MAP for frozen files. New entry points are adapters; scientific regression, full GPU inference and local CPU self-checks each have their own acceptance scope.
4. HISTORY retains stopped and unsuccessful routes. Preserve human judgments, unknown identities and matching-tie ranges as recorded.
5. Users send review answers separately for protocol-governed management. Unblinding follows explicit authorization, and the second reviewer supplies an independent human judgment.
6. Before public upload, review third-party licenses/attribution, author contributions, reproduction on an external machine, paper citations and pending two-person QC. LICENSE_CODE covers its stated project-code scope; third-party files and derived data follow their own licenses.
7. Run privacy and manifest checks again. A clean scan records the outcome of its specified patterns; broader confidentiality review remains necessary. Review each newly added file.
